# Safety, Claims, and Rights

## Rights gate

Confirm permission for every supplied or generated product image, package, logo, presenter likeness, voice, testimonial, music track, font, and reference clip. Do not infer redistribution rights from access, payment, or customer delivery.

Do not publish private briefs, customer assets, credentials, paid test renders, or confidential product information.

## Claim hierarchy

Use only:

- facts explicitly confirmed by the user;
- visual attributes plainly supported by authorized images;
- official material supplied by the user with permission.

Do not invent:

- specifications, material composition, certification, safety, medical benefit, environmental claim, price, warranty, availability, efficacy, performance magnitude, battery life, comfort, fit, durability, or superiority;
- ownership, purchase history, routine, preference, long-term use, or endorsement for a generated presenter;
- controls, hidden surfaces, detachable parts, or operating sequences based on category resemblance.

Sensory demonstrations may show an authorized use action and restrained reaction, but the reaction is not objective proof.

## Unsafe and regulated use

Stop or request verified instructions when operation could create physical, medical, electrical, chemical, ingestion, fire, child-safety, or legal risk. Do not improvise application areas, dosage, exposure, charging, disassembly, or protective claims.

## Output disclosure

State which facts were verified, which visible details were used, which unknowns were omitted, which assets were authorized, and which provider capabilities remain unverified. Clearly separate local schema validation from human audiovisual review and live-platform acceptance.
