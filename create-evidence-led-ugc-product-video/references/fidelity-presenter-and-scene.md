# Fidelity, Presenter, and Scene

## Product invariant sheet

Record only supported identity features:

- silhouette and proportions;
- supplied front, back, side, top, and bottom views;
- color blocks, finish, transparency, reflections, and material boundaries;
- label spelling, logos, buttons, ports, seams, closures, caps, accessories, and packaging geometry.

Mark unseen surfaces as unknown. Function confirmation does not establish the exact geometry of a hidden control, dispenser, connector, or closure.

## One-product continuity

Default to one target product unit. Do not let the presenter wear one copy while holding another. Do not remove sleeves, straps, caps, blades, applicators, or other parts unless their detachability is supported. Keep the same unit's state transition visible or logically continuous.

## Presenter choice

Preserve an authorized supplied presenter. For product-only input, explicitly confirm male-presenting, female-presenting, or open match before presenter generation.

For open match, record a non-gender creative basis such as audience, wardrobe, scene, activity, framing, or brand tone. Never map product category to gender, ethnicity, or nationality. Match explicit lawful market and casting requirements without treating a broad word such as “overseas” as an ethnicity.

## Lived-in UGC scene

Default to an ordinary, category-appropriate setting with depth, practical objects, natural light variation, and phone-camera framing. Avoid a blank wall, seamless studio sweep, luxury set, or ecommerce tabletop unless selected by the user.

Keep presenter, wardrobe, room, time of day, light direction, and product scale consistent across the four states.

## Critical-frame review

Inspect:

1. first product appearance;
2. closest product view;
3. first hand contact;
4. largest product motion or angle change;
5. hero action;
6. final use/result.

Compare silhouette, proportions, color, material, structure, label, logo, packaging, grip, and inventory against the authorized source.
